GAME RULES & PAYTABLE

CASINO LOBBY: Choose from 3-Reel (5 lines), 5-Reel (10 lines), or 5-Reel (20 lines) cabinets. Click LOBBY anytime to switch machines without losing balance or progress.

PAYLINES: Matches evaluate left-to-right. On 5-reel cabinets, payouts are awarded for combinations of 3, 4, or 5 matching symbols.

CASCADE & MULTIPLIER: Winning symbols explode and make way for new falling symbols! Consecutive cascade drops climb the multiplier trail: x1 ➔ x2 ➔ x3 ➔ x5 ➔ x10 ➔ x15 ➔ x20 ➔ x25!

FREE SPINS (3+ SCATTERS): Awards 10 Free Spins! The multiplier is STICKY — it never resets between spins and only climbs higher!

WILD (W): Substitutes for regular paying symbols. Wilds can land with x2, x3, or x5 Multipliers! Random Neon Lightning strikes can turn entire reels into Expanding Wilds!

SUPER SHOOTER BONUS: 3+ BONUS symbols trigger the arcade target shooter mini-game!

CYBER WHEEL & JACKPOTS: Fill the Cyber Energy Meter (50 spins / Quests) or reach a Must-Drop Jackpot limit to unlock the Cyber Wheel! When unlocked, an alert gives you a limited time window (35s) to claim your spins with up to x20 Mega multipliers!

GAMBLE (DOUBLE OR NOTHING): Available on base wins up to 25x bet. You have a 5-second window to double your win (Red or Black) up to a maximum streak of 5 consecutive rounds!
